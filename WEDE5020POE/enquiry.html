Do Your Part!

If you suspect a child is being abused or neglected, don't stay silent. Reporting your concerns could save a child's life. You don't need proof, just a genuine concern for a child's safety.

Ask for Help

Chubby Chums works closely with Child Protection Units and Child Treatment Services to investigate reports and get children the help they need. Every enquiry is treated with care and confidentiality.

If a child is in immediate danger, please contact the South African Police Service (10111) or Childline (0800 055 555) right away. Use the form below for non-emergency reports and enquiries.
